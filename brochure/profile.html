01 · Pre-Award

Tender, Pricing & Contract Readiness

جاهزية المناقصة والتسعير والموقف التعاقدي
What is usually going wrong
Teams start pricing before scope, obligations, interfaces and commercial exposures are fully understood - creating bid losses, hidden cost or weak margin after award.
يبدأ التسعير قبل وضوح نطاق الأعمال والالتزامات والمخاطر، فتظهر بنود غير محسوبة بعد الترسية أو يضعف هامش الربح.
What changes
A clearer bid position, protected margin, stronger commercial assumptions and better award decisions.
Typical intervention: scope & requirement analysis · bid/no-bid · risk/opportunity · pricing strategy · procurement/subcontract inputs · contract review · tender negotiation.
02 · Delivery

Project Recovery & Delivery Control

استعادة المشروع والسيطرة على التنفيذ
What is usually going wrong
The project is late, but recovery is treated as a planning exercise while engineering, procurement, cash, contracts, resources and interfaces continue to drive the delay.
المشروع يتأخر، لكن خطة الاسترداد تركز على البرنامج فقط بينما الأسباب الحقيقية موزعة بين الهندسة والمشتريات والسيولة والعقود والموارد والتنسيق.
What changes
One integrated recovery position, clear priorities, accountable actions and management visibility on what actually controls completion.
Typical intervention: delay root-cause analysis · recovery/acceleration · engineering & technical-office action plans · procurement recovery · commercial/claims actions · resource & interface control · management reporting.
03 · Business Performance

Procurement, Supply Chain & Performance Improvement

تحسين المشتريات وسلسلة الإمداد والأداء
What is usually going wrong
Long-lead items, fragmented supplier decisions, excess inventory, poor cash alignment or manual workflows start consuming time, working capital and management attention.
المواد طويلة التوريد، وقرارات الموردين المتفرقة، وتضخم المخزون، وضعف ربط الالتزامات بالسيولة والإجراءات اليدوية تبدأ في استنزاف الوقت والسيولة والإدارة.
What changes
More controlled supply, better cash visibility, lower operational friction and a practical roadmap for stronger team capability.
Typical intervention: procurement strategy · long-lead recovery · supplier negotiation · cash-flow planning · inventory optimization · supply-chain risk · workflow improvement · digitalization · KPI/capability development.
